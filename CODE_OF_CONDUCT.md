# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate respectfully and assume good intent while discussing evidence.
- Give specific, constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Welcome questions and support contributors with different experience levels.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and personal boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, apologise when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, or sustained bad-faith conduct.
- Misuse of project channels for unrelated promotion or abuse.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue. The project owner may remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and made without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
